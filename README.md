This is a recreation of Tic Tac Toe made in Unity for the 20 games challenge.

# Tic Tac Toe (Completed ✅)

- There are 9 fields, the players take turns, the player chooses a field, and then the AI chooses a random field too.
- The goal is to get 3 symbols (X/O) in a row, column, or diagonally. If the player or AI does this, a play again screen shows up with the ability to play again.
- I don't really have anything more to say as the concept of this game is really simple.
